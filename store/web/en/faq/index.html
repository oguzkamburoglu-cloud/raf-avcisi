About the game

What kind of game is Shelf Hunter?
A grocery shelf sorting puzzle. Every aisle has a shopping list; you bring the listed products together in threes on one shelf and finish the list before time runs out.

Is Shelf Hunter free?
Yes. The game is free to download and play. There are optional in-app purchases and optional rewarded ads; nothing is required to finish the aisles.

Does it work offline? Do I need an account?
You can play without an internet connection and without an account.

Where can I download it?
Shelf Hunter: Grocery Puzzle is on the App Store. In Türkiye it is listed as Raf Avcısı.

Inside an aisle

Is it worth matching products that are not on the list?
Trios that are not on the list do not finish the aisle, but they clear the shelf, which frees space and moves the back row forward. That helps when you are stuck; the list is still what ends the aisle.

What if I have no moves left?
If every shelf is full you cannot move a product. If the basket is unlocked, park a product there, or use undo or shuffle. You can also replay the aisle.

What is the basket for?
Once unlocked, the basket lets you set a product aside: tap the product, then the basket. It is the quickest way to open up a jammed shelf.

How do I open boxed products?
A boxed product cannot be moved. Each shelf you clear opens one box, so in aisles with boxes it pays to clear the easy trios first.

What do the red % tags mean?
Those products are on sale. Clearing their trio gives you extra time, so look for them first when the timer is low.

Score and helpers

How are stars earned?
The faster you finish the aisle, the more stars you earn. Shelves cleared back to back also score combo points.

What helpers are there?
Undo, hint and shuffle. They are limited, so save them for the hardest moments.

What is the daily list?
The same puzzle for everyone, new every day.
