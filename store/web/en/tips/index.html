Shelf Hunter tips: 9 tactics

The rule in Shelf Hunter is simple: bring three of the same product to one shelf, the shelf clears and the item on your list is ticked. The hard part is doing it before time runs out. These tactics come straight from the game's own rules.

1. Read the list before the shelves

Every aisle has a shopping list, and the list is what ends the aisle, not emptying every shelf. Chasing a trio that is not on the list costs time. Before your first move, find where the listed products sit.

2. Keep one shelf empty

You can only move a product to a shelf with a free spot. If every shelf fills up you have no moves left. Do not refill a shelf you cleared early; use it as working space to separate products.

3. Start with products that already have two together

If a shelf already holds two of the same product, bringing the third is a single move. These are the cheapest trios; collect them first to open up the shelves.

4. Remember the back row

When a shelf is cleared, the back row moves forward. If you cannot see the product you need, it is probably waiting behind. Clearing the shelf in front is often the only way to reach it.

5. Use the basket as a parking spot

Once the basket is unlocked, tap a product and then the basket to set it aside. Parking the one product that blocks the way costs fewer moves than carrying it from shelf to shelf. Do not forget it there; take it back when a spot opens.

6. Do not skip sale items

The product with the red % tag is on sale, and clearing its trio gives you extra time. When the clock is tight, finishing the sale trio before the next list item usually pays off.

7. Open boxes by clearing shelves

A boxed product cannot be moved. Each shelf you clear opens one box. In aisles with boxes, start with an easy trio to get the boxes opening.

8. Save helpers for the end

Undo, hint and shuffle are limited. They are worth the most late in an aisle, when time is short and one product is missing, not at the start. Finishing an aisle without helpers also counts as a mission.

9. Speed earns stars

Finishing the list is enough to pass the aisle, but speed decides the stars. Shelves cleared back to back score combo points. Once you know an aisle, replaying it is the easiest way to reach three stars.

For the daily list

The daily list is the same puzzle for everyone and changes every day. Finishing it three days in a row is a mission of its own, so keeping the streak is worth more than one high score.

Questions

Is Shelf Hunter free?
Yes. The game is free to download and play. There are optional in-app purchases and optional rewarded ads; nothing is required to finish the aisles.

What happens when time runs out?
The checkout closes with items still on your list. You can watch a short ad for extra time if you want, or replay the aisle.

Does it work offline?
Yes. You can play without an internet connection and without an account.
