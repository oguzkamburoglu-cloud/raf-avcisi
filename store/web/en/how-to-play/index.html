How to play Shelf Hunter

Shelf Hunter is a grocery shelf sorting puzzle. Every aisle has a shopping list; you bring the listed products together in threes and finish the list before time runs out. Here is everything to know before your first aisle.

One aisle, step by step

  1. Read the list. When the aisle opens, read the shopping list first. The list is what ends the aisle.
  2. Tap a product, then a shelf. Tap the product you want to move, then tap a shelf with a free spot. The product moves there.
  3. Bring three of the same to one shelf. When three identical products meet on a shelf, the shelf clears and the item on your list is ticked.
  4. Use the row that moves forward. When a shelf is cleared, the back row moves forward and new products appear.
  5. Finish the list before time runs out. When every item on the list is ticked, the aisle is done. The faster you are, the more stars you earn.

What you see on screen

Shopping list: The products you need to collect in that aisle. Trios that are not on the list do not finish the aisle.

Timer: Every aisle has a time limit. When it runs out, the checkout closes.

Basket: Once unlocked, use it to set a product aside: tap the product, then the basket.

Box: A boxed product cannot be moved. Each shelf you clear opens one box.

Red % tag: A product on sale. Clearing its trio gives you extra time.

Helpers: Undo, hint and shuffle. They are limited.

Stars and combos: Finishing fast earns stars; shelves cleared back to back score combo points.

Daily list: The same puzzle for everyone, new every day.

Questions

Is Shelf Hunter free?
Yes. The game is free to download and play. There are optional in-app purchases and optional rewarded ads; nothing is required to finish the aisles.

What if I have no moves left?
If every shelf is full you cannot move a product. If the basket is unlocked, park a product there, or use undo or shuffle. You can also replay the aisle.

Does it work offline?
Yes. You can play without an internet connection and without an account.
